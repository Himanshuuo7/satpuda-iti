## Organisation-level facts

- Operator: **Maharana Pratap Shikshan Samiti, Balaghat** (S1, S5). Some pages say "Satpuda Maharana Pratap Shikshan Samiti" (S3).
- Group start: **1999** (S1, S5). Campus pages give **15 Dec 1999** as the establishment date of the group (S4).
- Affiliation: NCVT, New Delhi; CTS scheme; DGET/DGT, Ministry of Labour (historic wording); administered by Directorate of Technical Education, Govt. of MP; "Recommended by Quality Council of India" (S3, S4, S5).
- Institute counts — **conflicting organisational claims, show attributed only**:
  - "20+ Institutes" (site footer, S1)
  - "15 ITI Campuses" (homepage counter, S5)
  - "18 institutes in 10 locations", "19+ group institutes" (S6, 2021; includes non-ITI institutions)
  - "22+ institutes managed over 23 years", "network of 18 trusted institutes in central India" (S7, 2022)
  - **Individually verifiable ITI records: 16** (S2). No further Satpuda ITIs found in S8.

### Principal data
Principals are taken from the official campus pages, which were last visibly updated ~2021–2022. They are shown
in the UI as "as listed on the official campus page" — **re-confirm with each campus before launch**.

### Intake data
Intake figures on campus pages are inconsistent in places (e.g. Manjhapur Electrician "36 units / 756", Mauganj
Electrician "36 units / 720", Itarsi "Total Capacity 6 / Intake 120"). They are stored as published but are **not
summed or shown as headline numbers** in the UI.

### Infrastructure tables
Several campus pages publish identical trade-infrastructure tables (Betul, Kundipura, Mandla share one; Manjhapur,
Rewa, Mauganj share another) — likely template reuse. Treated as per-campus claims only where unique; UI shows the
NCVT-norm style figures only for Betul-type table with an "as published" note. ⚠️

## Coordinates
Only Manjhapur has precise coordinates (official contact-page map embed). All other map points are **town-level
approximations** (public town centroids) used solely to place pins on the schematic map. Stored as
`mapPoint` with `precision: 'town'`, never displayed as numbers.

## Timeline (verified milestones only)
